Drawing from Nature

These drawings explore the patterns and structures found in nature through computational rules and processes. Written in code and drawn by a pen plotter, each work emerges from a set of instructions that shape the movement of the line, balancing order with variation and control with unpredictability.

Rather than simply depicting natural forms, the drawings investigate how complex patterns can emerge from relatively simple rules - and how computational processes can offer new ways of observing and interpreting the world.
