Connection

Connection is a browser-based audiovisual artwork exploring the hidden relationships that connect things to one another.

Particles move through a shared space, largely unseen until they draw close enough for connections to emerge. Lines appear, tones sound, and individual movements begin to form part of a larger audiovisual composition. As the work unfolds, these relationships accumulate, intensify and gradually recede.

The work invites us to consider how connections can exist beyond immediate perception and how something that seems isolated might be part of a much larger, interconnected whole.
