About

Simon's educational profile bridges the domains of Fine Art: Painting (BA, Glasgow School of Art), Communication Art & Design (MA, Royal College of Art) and Media Arts & Technology (PhD, Queen Mary University of London).

As a creative technologist he has worked both individually and collaboratively with the likes of United Visual Artists, D-Labs, Special Projects, Julie Freeman and Susan Collins. Simon's work has been exhibited as installations, digital artworks and innovative designs at places that include the Bath Music Festival, Oxford Christmas Light Festival, La Gaite Lyrique, Science Museum, Design Museum and GV Art Gallery.

Simon is employed as a Senior Lecturer in Digital Media in the School of Computing and Creative Technologies at the University of the West of England, Bristol, UK.
